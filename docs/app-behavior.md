# App behavior

[← README](../README.md)

## Daily rules and offline behavior

- Habits repeat daily, on selected weekdays, weekly, monthly, or every **1–365 days/weeks/months**. Weekly periods start Monday; monthly periods start on the 1st. Custom intervals use a chosen start date (aligned to its containing Monday/month start for weeks/months). A habit created partway through a calendar period gets the remaining days as its first period. Future starts remain unavailable until their start date.
- Longer-period habits appear in a separate Today section and do not increase the daily target. Complete once anytime before the period ends: the checkoff stays checked throughout that period and earns **10 XP once**. Streaks count consecutive successful occurrences; an unfinished current period stays open, and a missed finished period breaks the streak. Daily XP is shown on the original completion date.
- Today shows each habit's current streak and the selected date. Opening the page always starts on today. Use the day arrows to fill in missed checkoffs on dates when the habit was scheduled, up to **3 days before today** by default. Set `DAILY_HISTORY_DAYS` in the environment or root `.env` to a non-negative integer to change this limit (`0` allows today only), then restart the server or recreate the Compose container; no rebuild is needed. XP and streaks update automatically. Past checkoffs also work offline with the last cached limit.
- Habit interval edits take effect after the current day or longer period ends; further edits replace the pending change. A changed rhythm starts a new current streak while retaining earlier completions, best streaks, XP and rewards. Timezone changes also take effect after the current period, preserving its anchor and streak. Schedule history retains earlier rhythms and timezones. Legacy weekday history keeps its original streak behavior; new interval edits record an explicit reset marker. Dates use IANA timezones, not the server's local clock.
- Goals and their steps may be undated or have an optional date. Dated goals without active steps appear among Today’s due tasks; goals with steps are completed through those steps. Today shows due and overdue steps, plus a “Whenever you have time” section suggesting the first unfinished undated step from each active goal in its existing order. Expand it to show all available undated steps and check them off directly. Optional steps earn XP without increasing the daily progress total; completed steps stay visible on the day they were finished, including in daily history. Past days show completed optional steps only. An empty goal does not complete.
- Gifts (`/gifts`) and achievements (`/achievements`) have separate pages and navigation entries. The old `/rewards` URL redirects to Gifts.
- Gifts can be edited with a name, reward icon, up to 2,000 characters of multiline notes and clickable HTTP/HTTPS links, and an unlock milestone. The Gifts archive toggle shows archived gifts and their claim history. Received gifts are read-only and can still be archived. Locked gifts whose habit or goal was archived are marked as paused: the day page skips them as the next gift and links to Gifts, where they show a warning and can be given a new milestone, marked as received, or deleted. Gifts unlocked before their target was archived stay claimable. The day page lists gifts received on the selected day, using the profile timezone. Offline edits cannot overwrite received gift details during synchronization. Existing gifts use the gift icon and have no description until edited.
- Undo recalculates XP, levels, streaks, badges, and unclaimed gifts. Claimed gifts remain in history. Archiving retains completion records and earned XP.
- After the first connected visit, install the app through your browser. On iOS use **Share → Add to Home Screen**.
- Habits, goals, steps, and gifts with no log history can be deleted after confirmation. Items with checkoffs (including undone checkoffs) or claimed gifts can only be archived, preserving their history and XP. A goal also retains the history of its steps. Deleting an unused habit or goal deletes its unclaimed linked gifts. If another device logs an item while it is being deleted, sync archives it and keeps that log. Upgrades delete archived items without history from server storage and browser caches.
- After the first connected profile visit, every feature except switching profiles and notification setup/delivery works offline: create/edit/delete or archive habits, goals and gifts, create/edit/delete or archive steps, reorder steps, check off or undo tasks, claim gifts, and save settings or avatars. Changes are committed to IndexedDB before the UI confirms success and survive reloads. Onboarding can finish offline once the initial profile has loaded. A new profile cannot initialize without that first connection.
- Local changes, reconnection, foreground focus, app launch, and manual retry trigger background synchronization. While the page is visible, idle sync checks every 5 seconds bring in changes from other devices without a refresh; hidden pages check every 60 seconds and sync immediately when brought forward. Cached startup and local saves never wait for API availability. Background synchronization runs without inline status hints on tasks or banners for offline, queued, or slow syncs; sync status remains available in Settings → View sync log. Requests time out after 10 seconds; failures retry with backoff and do not interrupt edits with network error dialogs. Background Sync support is not required. Retries are idempotent, and duplicate wins earn XP once.
- **Settings → View sync log** shows the last 50 sync operations for the current profile on this device, including timestamps, durations, pending change counts, retries, and outcomes. The log persists in IndexedDB and can be viewed offline.
- Browser-ID switches keep local documents, sync baselines, pending changes, and sync logs scoped to their original ID. Pending changes are never replayed into another account. IndexedDB transactions coordinate local writes across tabs; server revision checks coordinate concurrent uploads without holding browser locks during network requests.
- Sync performs a three-way merge against the last synchronized document. Independent additions and edits to different fields combine; the server value wins when both devices edit the same field differently. Completed wins conflicting completion changes, including an undo versus a remote undo/recompletion. Ordinary undo still works when the server completion has not changed since the baseline. Archives and gift claims survive conflicts. Gift unlock rules merge together, with the server winning conflicting milestone edits; independent gift notes and icon edits still combine until a gift is received. Receiving a gift freezes its details, with the server’s received copy taking precedence during conflicts. Schedule entries merge by effective date, with the server winning ties; timezone conflicts keep the server calendar schedules together. Step ordering preserves additions, using server ordering when both sides reorder. Original completion dates and timezones are retained.
- Existing browser caches upgrade without clearing profiles or pending checkoffs. Unreplayable legacy operations remain saved for recovery; **Use server progress** explicitly discards pending local changes and reloads saved data.
- Updates are prompted and cannot be activated while changes are pending. Reduced-motion preferences disable decorative motion and celebrations.

Browser storage is a local cache, not a backup. Unsynced changes depend on that browser's IndexedDB storage remaining available.

## Reminders

Habits, goals, and steps each have optional reminder times. Goal and step reminders use the profile timezone, repeat daily until completion, and start on the optional due date when one is set. Archived tasks and steps of archived goals do not send reminders. Enable notifications separately on each device in Settings. An evening check-in reminder is sent once a day when at least one habit is still open today and nothing (no habit, goal, or step) has been checked off by the chosen time. It is on by default at 20:00 in the profile timezone; turn it off or change the time under **Settings → Notifications**. Like other reminders, it reaches every device where notifications are enabled.

## Challenge icons

Choose an icon when creating or editing a habit, goal, or step. Icons appear on the Today page and in their lists, keep the selected color (steps use their goal’s color), and sync across devices. Existing items keep their leaf or target icon until you select another.

## Custom avatars

Choose **Edit avatar** in Settings to customize skin tone, hair, facial features, clothing, accessories, and background in a live preview. Randomize a look or reset your changes, then save the avatar. During onboarding, the avatar is saved when you finish your profile. Existing robot avatars remain until you save a custom portrait.

## Plant growth

The Today plant grows into a rounded bush in 24-level branches: six levels add one leaf each, then the next eighteen add one blossom petal each, completing three six-petal blossoms. Petal levels do not add normal leaves. Once a branch is full, the next level begins a new branch spreading outward and upward through the crown. Blossoms sit over the foliage, and the whole plant zooms out uniformly as the bush fills out. Growth respects the device's reduced-motion setting.
